Art & audio wanted

Everything Societies looks and sounds like today is generated by scripts: simple low-poly models, flat-colored terrain, synthesized sounds. Here is every asset that would make it a real game. There are 208 items, and 119 of them don't exist at all yet. Each comes with a prompt ready to paste into an image, 3D, music or sound generator, or to hand to an artist.

Download ASSETS.md
Every prompt asks for original work: no copying of existing games' art, characters, logos or music. Wonders are inspired by real, public-domain landmarks. Finished files go at the listed paths, then the content files point at them.

Unit models

One per unit type; the game tints the base in the owner's color.
